# Hotel_Booking_Analysis


## Project Overview

The hospitality industry generates a large amount of booking and customer-related data every day. Understanding customer behavior, booking trends, cancellation patterns, and revenue fluctuations is essential for hotels to improve operational efficiency and maximize profitability. 

This project presents an in-depth Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) of hotel booking data using Python. The analysis focuses on identifying meaningful business insights from customer reservations made across city hotels and resort hotels. By applying data cleaning, preprocessing, and visualization techniques, the project uncovers patterns related to booking demand, customer preferences, seasonal trends, pricing behavior, and cancellation risks.

The dataset contains more than 119,000 hotel booking records with over 30 features, including booking status, lead time, average daily rate (ADR), customer type, market segment, arrival details, and stay duration. The primary objective of this analysis is to transform raw booking data into actionable insights that can support data-driven decision-making in the hospitality sector.

---

## Objectives of the Project

- Analyze hotel booking behavior and customer preferences
- Identify major factors contributing to booking cancellations
- Compare booking patterns between city hotels and resort hotels
- Understand seasonal demand and revenue trends
- Study the impact of lead time on cancellation probability
- Explore customer segmentation and booking distribution
- Generate business insights through visual analytics

## Key Findings and Business Insights

### 1. Higher Cancellation Rate in City Hotels
The analysis revealed that city hotels experienced significantly higher booking cancellations compared to resort hotels. This indicates that customers booking city hotels may have more flexible travel plans or higher booking uncertainty.

### 2. Lead Time Strongly Influences Cancellations
Customers who booked hotels several months in advance were more likely to cancel their reservations. Long lead times were positively associated with cancellation probability.

### 3. Seasonal Demand Impacts Revenue
The Average Daily Rate (ADR) showed noticeable fluctuations across different months. Peak travel seasons resulted in higher ADR values, indicating increased customer demand and dynamic pricing strategies.
